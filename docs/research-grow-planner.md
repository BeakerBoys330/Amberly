Source basis: Resource Innovation Institute (RII) best-practice guides, GrowWeedEasy, Royal Queen Seeds, Fast Buds, General Hydroponics & FoxFarm charts, University of Guelph (2023) and NC State (2020) photoperiod studies, Aroya VPD chart. Most grower numbers come from commercially motivated vendors — treat as consensus defaults and let user logs adjust.

## Timelines
- Autos: 8–12 weeks seed to harvest (fast ~8–9, sativa-leaning up to 13–14). Self-flower weeks 3–5. Prefer breeder's stated days.
- Photos: veg 3–8 weeks (user choice); flower indica 7–9 wk, hybrid 8–10, sativa 10–14. Stretch after flip 1.5–3× (default 2×).
- Flip rule (GrowWeedEasy): flip at ½ desired final height (⅓ for stretchy sativas, ¾ for compact indicas).
- 12/12 is convention: Guelph 2023 found flowering initiates up to 14 h light in all tested cultivars; NC State 2020 found 16 h blocks flower development. Light leaks delay flowering.
- Outdoor: flowering starts as day length drops below ~14–14.5 h (late July–mid Aug at 35–45°N). Day length = (24/π)·arccos(−tan φ·tan δ), δ = 23.44°·sin(360/365·(284+N)). Warn if harvest falls after first frost.

## Watering
- Signals: pot feels light, top 1–2" dry, water to 10–20% runoff (minimal runoff for living/organic soil). Never let coco dry out.
- Volume ≈ 25–33% of pot. Seedlings: small ring waterings (e.g. 500 ml in a 5 gal).
- Frequency: soil every 2–3 days (hard plastic 5 gal: 1–2×/week); coco every 1–2 days. Peak thirst in stretch/mid flower. Learn from logs.
- Overwatering: droop with heavy pot, firm curled leaves, gnats. Underwatering: limp papery droop, light pot.
- Autos: start in final pot (3–5 gal typical). Photos: cup → 1 gal → 3–7 gal.

## Feeding
- Start at ½ label strength; autos ¼–½. Soil: plain water first 2–3 weeks (3–4 for hot soils like Ocean Forest). Coco/hydro: feed from first water.
- Cap default EC ~1.8 soil / 2.0 coco; warn > 2.2.
- Cal-Mag: always in coco, routinely with RO/soft water or LED.
- GH Flora 3-part: EC 0.4–0.5 wk1 → 1.4–1.7 late veg/mid bloom → 0.9–1.1 late bloom → 0.6–0.8 ripen.
- FoxFarm soil trio: Big Bloom from wk1, Grow Big from wk3, Tiger Bloom from wk6; growers report burn at chart rate → default ½.
- Flushing is contested: only controlled trial (Rx Green 2019, coco, one cultivar, nutrient-company funded) found no benefit; Sensi Seeds still recommends it. Offer as choice.
- Deficiency cues: N def = yellowing from lower leaves up; N toxicity = dark glossy clawed leaves; Ca = rusty spots on new/middle leaves; Mg = interveinal yellowing on older leaves; burn = crispy tips; pH lockout = many deficiencies at once (check runoff pH first); light burn = bleaching only near the light.

## Training
- Topping 4–6 nodes, veg only, last ≥10 days pre-flip. FIM 3–5 nodes. Supercrop late veg.
- Autos: LST from ~week 3; top at most once before first pistils (~day 14–25); avoid HST after flowering starts.
- Defoliation at day 21 of flower is popular but labeled "folklore" by some — optional/advanced.

## Light
- Auto schedule: 18/6 default (Fast Buds), 20/4 "push" (yield claims are marketing, not trials), 24/0 for cold rooms. DLI matters more than hours.
- Ramp PPFD ≤ 100–150 every 3–5 days. Light stress: bleaching/tacoing tops only.
- LED draw: ~20 W/sq ft veg minimum, ~30 W/sq ft flower default, 40–50 high performance.

## Climate alerts
Temp > 85 °F warn / > 90 critical; < 65 °F warn / < 60 critical. VPD < 0.4 or > 1.6 danger. Flower RH > 55% warn (botrytis can start in 24–48 h in dense buds), > 60–65% critical. Leaf temp ≤ dew point always critical. Drying > 70 °F or < 50% RH = too fast; > 65% RH = mold risk.

## Harvest & trichome checks
- Start scans ~2 weeks before projected finish; every 3 days, then daily once cloudy > 50%.
- Sample calyx trichomes (not sugar leaves) from top, middle, and lower buds.
- Pistil darkening (50–70%) is a "start checking" cue, never the decision.
- 24–48 h pre-harvest darkness: optional, anecdotal.
