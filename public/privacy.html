Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Amberly is operated by BeakerBoys ("we", "us").

In short: Amberly keeps your grows, notes, photos, and readings on your own device. Amberly works fully without an account. If you choose to share, you decide separately whether your followers can see it and whether BeakerBoys can use it to train Amberly. You can change or delete it anytime. We do not use ads or analytics, and we do not sell personal information.

1. What stays on your device

Amberly saves the following in your browser's storage on your device:

  • Grows, watering/feeding/climate logs, and notes you enter
  • Photos you choose to add (resized), and trichome readings from photos you analyze
  • A record that you accepted these terms (date and version), and your sign-in session if you have an account

Amberly analyzes photos on your device. Amberly does not send any of this to us unless you share it.

2. Accounts (optional)

You only need an account to share or follow. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code (no password). We store your email, the date you accepted these terms, and, if you use following, a username and whether your profile is public. Other users see your username, never your email.

3. If you choose to share

Sharing is always your choice, item by item, with two separate switches:

  • Followers can see this: the followers you approve can see it and react with an emoji. If you make your profile public, any signed-in Amberly user who finds your profile can see it. Amberly never shows it in a feed to people who do not follow you.
  • Help train Amberly: BeakerBoys may use it to train and improve Amberly's trichome reading and grow guidance. BeakerBoys does not use content that you share only with followers for training.

What Amberly sends:

  • A trichome reading: the reading (clear/cloudy/amber percentages), strain name, type, breeder, your effect goal, your note, and the photos you analyzed.
  • A grow: its setup (strain, plant type, medium, pot and tent size, light, key dates), journal entries, and photos. Your nickname for the grow stays on your device. While a grow is shared, Amberly sends new entries automatically.
  • Photo details removed: before upload, Amberly redraws each photo to remove the location (GPS), camera model, and time information. Amberly sends only the photo itself.

Following data: we store who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, your emoji reactions, the users you block, and any reports you file. We store this data so the app can work and so we can keep the community safe. BeakerBoys staff can access shared content to review reports and enforce our rules.

4. Where data is stored

Our database provider, Supabase, stores shared data in the United States. Access rules ensure other users can only see what you shared with them, and only you can change or delete your data. Our hosting provider, Vercel, delivers the app. Like any website, both receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of each request.

When you sign in, a bot check from Cloudflare (Turnstile) runs first to stop automated sign-ups and spam. Cloudflare receives technical information about your browser and device, such as your IP address, to tell people from bots. Cloudflare says that it does not use this information for advertising. The check only loads when you open the sign-in screen.

Our email provider, Google (Gmail), sends the sign-in code. Google receives your email address to deliver that message.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking. Amberly sets no cookies. The Cloudflare bot check at sign-in can keep a small file in your browser to do its work.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, or name
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising, as defined by California law (CCPA/CPRA)

6. Stopping sharing and deleting data

  • Stop sharing a reading or grow at any time (turn both switches off). Amberly then deletes the shared copy. If you delete a shared item in the app, Amberly also deletes the shared copy.
  • Remove followers, cancel follows, and block users anytime from Following → Followers.
  • About → Account → Delete account & shared data deletes your account and everything that you shared.
  • About → Delete all data on this device deletes what Amberly stored on this device.

We keep shared data until you delete it or your account, or until we no longer need it to improve Amberly. Models already trained on it may keep what they learned, but we delete your photos and records.

7. Age

Amberly is only for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe a minor has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. You can delete it yourself in the app, or contact us for anything else.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your device is only as private as the device: anyone who can unlock it and open the app can see it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and ask you to accept again in the app.
